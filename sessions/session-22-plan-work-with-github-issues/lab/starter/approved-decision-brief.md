# Approved Decision Brief: Clear Request Status

## Outcome

Employees who submitted a facilities or equipment request can understand its current public status and expected next step without contacting support.

## In scope

- Plain-language public status
- Last public update time
- Expected next step
- Facilities and equipment requests
- Safe empty and stale states

## Out of scope

- Email, chat, or mobile notifications
- Internal notes
- Staff names
- Changes to the service workflow
- New reporting dashboards

## Acceptance evidence

- Requesters can see only their own requests.
- Every supported request displays a public status or an explicit unavailable state.
- Internal notes and staff-only status details are not exposed.
- The product owner approves the public status mapping.
- Service operations reviews the wording and stale-state behavior.

## Ownership

- **Decision owner:** Product owner
- **Delivery reviewer:** Delivery lead
- **Service reviewer:** Service operations manager
- **Access reviewer:** Privacy or security owner

## Approval

**Status:** Approved for issue planning
