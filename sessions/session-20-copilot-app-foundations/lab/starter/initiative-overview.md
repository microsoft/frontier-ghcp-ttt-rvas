# Service Request Portal Initiative

## Product

The Service Request Portal lets employees submit internal service requests and track their progress. Service coordinators route each request to the responsible team.

## Current problem

Requesters often contact support because the portal shows internal status names that do not explain what happens next.

## Proposed outcome

Show a plain-language status, the most recent public update, and the expected next step for each submitted request.

## Known constraints

- The first release covers facilities and equipment requests.
- Internal notes and staff-only status details must remain hidden.
- The existing service system remains the source of record.
- The product owner must approve the public status mapping.

## Open decision

The team has not decided whether the first release should include outbound notifications.
